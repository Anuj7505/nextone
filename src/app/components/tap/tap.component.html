TAP SPRING

Smart IoT-based Plant Watering System – Designed & Developed in India. Water your plants automatically and efficiently even when you are away.

Problem Area

Long holidays, WFH, or lockdown situations often lead to unavailability of individuals to water plants. Many times family members forget watering, resulting in plant deaths. TAP SPRING solves this for homes, offices, public places, and more – ensuring water saving and timely care.

What is Tap Spring?

TAP SPRING is a small IoT sprinkling device that automates watering of household plants, gardens, and vertical setups. Operable via mobile app or automated timer, it also supports 100W bulb provision for plant heating.

  • Mobile-triggered & auto time-based watering
  • Easy to install, no technical skills required
  • Works for pots, gardens & vertical setups
  • Eco-friendly, robust, India-designed solution
