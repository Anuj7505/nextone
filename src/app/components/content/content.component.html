Frequently Asked Questions

What is content moderation?

Content moderation is the process of monitoring, reviewing, and filtering user-generated content to prevent the spread of harmful or inappropriate material.

How does AI improve content moderation?

AI automates detection of hate speech, harassment, and explicit content with high accuracy, reducing manual effort and response time.

Can moderation be multilingual?

Yes! Our moderation tools support multiple languages, ensuring safe communication across diverse global audiences.

Is human review still required?

Yes — while AI handles large-scale detection, human reviewers are essential for nuanced judgment and sensitive cases.
