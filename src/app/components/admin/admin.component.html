Overview

1Admin is a powerful mobile and web-based platform designed to manage Employee Attendance, Site Operations, and Task Tracking. It’s ideal for industries like Construction, Banking, BFSI, Sensitive Document Delivery, and Maintenance Services. The system ensures accuracy, transparency, and efficiency in workforce and site management.
