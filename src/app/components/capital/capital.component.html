LOAN MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (LMS)

This module describes all key functionalities of the Loan Management System (LMS), including customer management, loan product management, loan application processing, and others.

Define Customer Types

Ability to define different types of customers such as proprietorship firm, partnership firm, private company, individual, limited company, self-help group (SHG), trust, NGO, etc.

Capture Unique Identifiers

Ability to capture unique identifiers of customers such as PAN card, TIN number, GST, company registration number, etc.

Capture and Modify Multiple Addresses

Ability to capture and modify multiple addresses of the customer such as registered office, communication address, office address, residential address, etc.

Link with Google Maps

Ability to link the customer's address with Google Maps.

Capture Promoter Details

Ability to capture details of promoters, partners, directors, members such as name, address, photo, signature, etc.

Capture and Modify Contact Details

Ability to capture and modify multiple contact details such as phone number, mobile number, email, website, etc.

Bank Account Details

Ability to capture bank account details.

Capture Financial Details

Ability to capture financial details/net worth of customers and individual persons.

Modify Customer Profile

Ability to modify and change customer profiles such as address, contact number, etc.

Upload Documents

Ability to upload photographs, KYC documents, balance sheets, etc.

Customer Communication

Ability to communicate with the customer via SMS, email, etc., and maintain their history.

Approval of Customer Entries

Maker and approval of customer entries and modification of customer profiles.

Restriction on Duplicate Entries

Ability to restrict duplicate customer entries.

Maintain Audit Trail

Maintaining history/audit trail for any changes in customer profiles.
