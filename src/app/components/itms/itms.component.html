The Concept

With the rapid increase in the number of vehicles, traditional methods of managing traffic are no longer effective. STMS integrates modern IT tools and Artificial Intelligence with traffic signals using CCTV feeds and deep learning algorithms. It enhances the monitoring and control of traffic by dynamically adjusting signals based on real-time vehicle density.

About the Project

Due to rapid urbanization and vehicle growth, authorities aim to implement an Intelligent Traffic Management System to provide greater control and insights for smooth and safe mobility. The main goal is to reduce traffic congestion and provide a faster, more enjoyable commute experience.

Objective of the Project

The primary objective of STMS is to integrate technology-driven prevention, regulation, and enforcement systems to improve mobility, discipline, and safety on city roads. By leveraging AI, machine vision, and analytics, the system continuously monitors and optimizes traffic flow to enhance road efficiency and safety.
