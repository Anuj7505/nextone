Key Capabilities

Comprehensive modules to run your institute end-to-end.

Student Management

Admission workflows, pre-admission marketing (bulk SMS & email), short-code integration, student profiles, timetable, attendance, assignments, exams & result analysis.

Employee & HR

Employee profiles, attendance (biometric integration), leave management, payroll & reporting.

Administration & Accounts

Admin dashboards, recruitment onboarding, salary & payroll management, accounts reports (TDS/advances), and MIS reporting.

Implementation Time: 3–5 months (module dependent), Testing: 1 month, Full Rollout: ~6 months (end-to-end).

  • RAM: 4GB+, HDD: 500GB+
  • Visual Studio/.NET stack (where needed), NAS backup, optional biometrics.
